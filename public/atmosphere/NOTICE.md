# Atmosphere art NOTICE

## What

Files under `public/atmosphere/` are **original** decorative assets for the GwentDeck UI fantasy-mix experiment:

- `forest-keep.webp`, `forest-path.webp` — AI-generated atmospheric landscapes (medieval fantasy dusk / misty forest road)
- `keep-silhouette.svg`, `ornament-flourish.svg` — hand-authored SVG ornaments

They are **not** scans, extracts, or lookalike rips of CD PROJEKT RED / official Gwent / Witcher Wiki art. No distinctive CDPR character likenesses or official card compositions.
